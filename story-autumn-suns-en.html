"Autumn suns" 💕

Author: Olga Kobylska | Translator: Helen Kapshytar | Theme: Autumn Romance & Yellow Chrysanthemums 💕

Well, it's been a wonderful summer! It's the beginning of June, the weather is great! And life is in full swing! I like to be busy with something: now here, now there. To be constantly on the move: friends, guests, acquaintances, meetings... At work, the last days before the vacation charged me with energy and cheerfulness.

I work as a teacher. So, on the last day of school practice, I decided to take my sixth-graders on a hike to the river. The morning was washed with fresh dew, and we were already stomping along the path, loaded with heavy backpacks. Why had they taken so much stuff – we went for half a day?

All the bags had already been sorted, the “tables” had been set, the fire had been lit. The children were doing their own business: some were busy playing games, some were toasting bread and sausages over the fire, some were throwing pebbles into the water. The boys decided to build a hut. There was a time for me to sit down to sunbathe on a blanket for a minute.

- Anya Viktorovna! – Alina and Katyushka came running, smiling mysteriously. - Those two guys are observing you….

In the clearing, except us, there were two married couples, an elderly woman with two poodles, and two young people. But without glasses I couldn’t see well, and I couldn’t see them well. And I constantly tried to keep an eye over all seventeen students at once. That’s why the “guys” were left without due attention.

Of course, a young pretty teacher in only a swimsuit and with wild eyes, who was wandering around the clearing, jumping and squeaking, playing with children, could not become unnoticed.

When, having bathed for 15 minutes, I was making my swimmers ashore with feigned severity, a pleasant voice came from behind:
- Lady, what are you doing tonight? Are you free?

Just in time. Especially when Vovka and Petka again plopped into the water, and I was standing all wet and with idiotic ponytails on my head, which my girls tied for me an hour ago. But before I had time to turn around, a voice belonging to a very handsome and tall guy (he stood so close that a small scar was visible above his left eyebrow) continued:
“Sorry it’s not on time, but we have to go now,” his friend was helping my boys to secure the last branches above the hut. - My name is Andrew. I won’t forgive myself if I don’t see such a charming girl again.

My students, who at one point became very obedient and busy, moved deeper into the clearing to play ball. They are very cunning (they have already tried to find me a pair for three times). We agreed to meet. Of course, after the hike I really wanted to go to bed, but the new acquaintance and the upcoming date seemed like an interesting adventure. First, I called my friend Olya, a biology teacher from school # 3. It turned out that she was also free in the evening and would not mind joining our company. And then I took a bath to clean from the camp dust. At the same time, I was thinking about what to dress up in.

The new acquaintances turned out to be normal guys. They were from a neighboring city and came to our town on business. Their car broke down, and while it was being repaired, the guys were sunbathing in our clearing.

Less than half an hour had passed, and we were talking like old friends. First, we sat in a cafe, and then went for a walk along the embankment.

The stars splashed quietly in the gentle waves. Andrei laid a court to me unobtrusively: he gave me a flower, then he supported me so that I didn’t stumble, or he threw a jacket over my shoulders. Sergei apparently liked Olya - they were a little behind us, talking about something funny, laughing.

Andrey sat down on the parapet:
– You have such an interesting city – there are steps all around, up and down.
- It’s just the bank of the Dnieper is so steep. And the local view from the upper floors is beautiful!

Then we walked in silence. Andrey held my hand. First, we took Olya home, and then returned to my entrance, where their car was. Sergei sat into the car - he had to return home. Andrey stayed to say goodbye:
– You are a real miracle! It's so good that I have met you. See you tomorrow!

He quickly kissed me on the lips and jumped into the car.
- See you tomorrow! – Going to bed, I wished him good night in my mind.

Tomorrow it would be my last day of work. And in the evening, I had to go to a kids’ holiday camp. There were so many things that needed to be done: get vacation pay, do the shopping, run to my brother’s work, and also go to my friend’s house to discuss yesterday’s event... Andrey found me at home around three o'clock in the afternoon: - Hello! Let's meet today? - Andryush, I’m leaving in the evening for the Chaika camp... - Let me give you a lift. As soon as the boss letsme go, I’ll come to you, okay? - I'll be waiting.

I waited until the last bus, and then I grabbed my bags and went to the stop. It was very annoying and I wanted to cry. I guess I had fallen in love...

In the bustle of the camp, my longing for my one-day acquaintance subsided a little. At the end of the first shift, I came home to rest for a couple of days. Then again - into battle, to the "Seagull"!

- Anyuta, a Andrey called you three or four times. He has asked when you’ll be there,” my mother recalled as she walked me to the bus.

My heart began to ache unpleasantly. Alas, I was leaving again.
- Tell him to call me at the camp.

And again the camp cycle: beaches, games, discos, night outings of the counselors. It seemed fun, but I really wanted to hear Andrei’s voice, and even better, to see him.

“Listen,” called Lenka, today’s duty officer, “a guy called you today, Andrey, I think. Twice. But there was no one to call you.

I angrily threw a pillow at her. It's like a bad luck! Apparently it was not to be fated.

It's school time. Lessons, registers, programs... I hadn’t heard a word from Andrey. Only Olya knew my melancholy.
- Do you want me to find him?
- How?! I don’t know anything, no last name, no place of work, no phone number!

All that remains was to sigh and cry quietly at night.

It was the Teacher's Day at school. Hooray! I needed to sort out the notebooks and gifted bouquets home, and then I would go to the gala party. On the way I met my mother:
- Annushka, your summer Andrei has just called you. About ten minutes ago. He congratulated you on the holiday!

I almost burst into tears. What was wrong?! Why didn’t we have a chance to meet?!
- Anyutka, is everything okay? Then I ran to Yegorovna.

It was clear that I did not go to any holiday. There was no mood. I made the bed and crawled under the blanket. I would wait. It started to rain. I didn’t want to go anywhere. Tears dropped down of my eyes. I woke up when it was already getting dark outside. A half past seven. He didn't call. Well, let. I turned on the lights and TV. I looked at the screen and saw a footage of a summer day flying by...

- Tir-r-r-l! – the entrance bell sang.

Mom probably forgot her keys. I opened the lock and froze.

Andrei stood at the door. My long-awaited person with such dear eyes.

Shaking the raindrops off his head, he smiled:
- Bad time again? I was passing by and I saw light in your window, like the sun among the clouds. Can I warm up?

I nodded my head happily and pushed my wet nose into his sweater.

– Have I told that you are a miracle? Only now I won’t let you go anywhere! – he held out a bouquet of huge yellow chrysanthemums. - Look at the little suns.

The room immediately became warm and cozy.
