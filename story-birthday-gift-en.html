"Birthday Gift" 💓

Author: Olga Kobylska | Translator: Helen Kapshytar | Theme: Love Letter, Prague & Birthday Surprise 💓

The week has come to an end. And to celebrate another successful contract of our company, Lilka, my co-worker, and I went to the summer cafe “Blue Bird” located on the bank of the Dnieper.

- God, how wonderful! – I stretched in my chair, slowly sipping my cocktail and observing the multi-colored lights on the ship passing by.

“Yes, it was a hot week,” Lilka meant both the bunch of things we had done and July, which was generous for heat.

Finally, the long-awaited coolness descended on the city with the coming twighlights.

Suddenly the mobile phone started beeping, interrupting our dreams for the weekend. - Hello! This is Pavel! Are you still there? “Boss...” I quietly answered Lilka to the question frozen in her eyes. She smiled meaningfully: they were chatting at the company that he was not indifferent to me. I don’t know - I haven’t noticed. - Yes? Well, we'll be there now! “They’ll be arriving now,” I reported.

Fifteen minutes later a familiar blue Opel appeared.

– Let me introduce from now on my right-hand man is our executive director – Oleg Ivanovich Danilov. Including my best friend! “Pavel recommended to us the young man who came with him.

– Just, Oleg.

As soon as I looked at him, I felt that my heart dropped somewhere down, and that someone had blocked my breathing. Yes, this was the real Alain Delon. I understand my grandmother, who had been watching her favorite French films with the blue-eyed brunette for hundreds of times, and then quietly sighed looking at him.

“And we are Lilies,” everyone was always surprised when my friend and I called our identical names.

- Then let's make wishes? – Oleg joked.

- Okay, stand between us and hold our hands tightly! – Lilya laughed. - Make a wish!

Oleg’s pleasant touch made my fingers feel so hot that an imperceptible spark, having pierced my body, illuminated my cheeks with a light blush.

Pavel, on seeing my embarrassment, decided to ease the situation:
– What do our wonderful flowers, the charming Lilies, want?
“Ice cream with champagne,” the friend decided not to think for a long time.
“And I want a knight on a white horse and a real castle,” I decided to puzzle our companions.
“Nothing could be simpler,” Pavel laughed. – In a week we are going on a business trip to the Czech Republic. There are plenty of castles there – observe as many as you want. Well, look at me, am I not a knight?
-Can you ride a horse? – Lilya doubted, laughing...

And so, in a good mood, the evening passed.

And at night I had a wonderful dream - about a beautiful knight with blue eyes.

At work everything went as usual. Oleg often came to our office, looked through the papers, which I had prepared, repeatedly, and even went to lunch with us at the cafe across the street several times. He turned out to be a good, fair chef.

Although I tried not to show how glad I was to see him, my burning eyes gave me away completely.

- What, friend, he got you? – Lilya hugged me understandingly.
- Yes…

Even the upcoming trip abroad was perceived without due enthusiasm. If Oleg had come with us...

During two weeks spent in Prague, we managed to resolve all financial matters, held many business meetings and, of course, wandered through ancient fairy-tale places.

Everything was going well, only a grey melancholy lurked in the corners of my eyes. I really missed my hometown, especially Oleg, with whom I was in love head over heels.

Even Pavel's attention and jokes could not make forget him.

“Look at the beauty you got,” Pasha handed me a garnet necklace. – It’s your birthday today! Congratulations! – He kissed me on the cheek.
- Pashka, you are great!
- But of course! I even read that garnet is your birthstone.
“Yes, Leos love jewelry,” I smiled. - Pasha, I love you so much! Like a brother!
“Well, at least like a brother, that’s okay,” he sighed falsely. – By the way, Oleg asked me to give you something.

Pavel began rummaging into his closet.

- Here, I solemnly present it! And go and get some rest already! There’s no point in sitting in office chairs on a day like this.

I, carefully pressing the pink bag to my chest, went to the hotel. In the room, without hesitation, I immediately began to unwrap the shiny paper of the package...

Oh, what a fluffy lion cub, oh, how cute! I hugged the soft toy tightly and closed my eyes in happiness. He had not forgotten. Oh, Oleg, Oleg...

Oh, what else is this? A small holiday card and a white sheet folded in four - a letter - fell out of the envelope enclosed in the package.

“Hello, Lilya! Let the lion cub become your guardian talisman and fulfill all your cherished dreams! Happy birthday!!! I would like to be with you this day, to see you, to say the best words to you! How I miss you! And I haven’t seen you for just one evening. I can’t imagine how I’ll survive two weeks when you go on a business trip... I constantly think of you: when I go to work in the morning, when I scroll through financial transactions in my head, when I see your serious face bent over papers, and at night... I just go crazy!When we first met, I immediately realized that I couldn’t live without you! P.S. Can I hope for responce? »

I immediately dialed the phone number. Beep, two, three...
- Honey, is that you? Happy birthday!

It was the best gift! It was a wonderful birthday!
