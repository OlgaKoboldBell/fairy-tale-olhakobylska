"Clock of love" 💗

Author: Olga Kobylska | Translator: Helen Kapshytar | Theme: Destiny, Fountains & Romance 💗

Well, that's all: the last boulevard - and home! Rest!

I carefully translated every word of the tour guide and, insensibly, began to get the meaning of the words. The unknown city brought pleasure not only to the cheerful French - its power and ancient calm made a huge impression on me as well.

– This wonderful overpass of fountains ends with an original construction. As you can see, it is an antique clock. Please note that its dials, facing in all directions, show different time. Let's try to guess which one is correct...

This was followed by an interesting story about the loving couple, the result of which was the expression “lovers take no account of time.” Everyone paid special attention to the belief associated with the “Clock of Love”: one needs to throw a coin into the fountain and make a wish.

At the same time, multi-colored coins sparkled over the water, reflecting the rays of the sun.

I should make a wish for myself. With a stupid expression on my face, I tossed the snout, aiming at the very middle of the seething stream. But, instead of falling safely into the water, my coin jingled the other flying coin and bounced back. So, well, let's see who is impeding with fulfilling my wish?

A handsome young man with a guilty smile held me a coin:
- Come on, let's try again.
“No,” I started being angry, “try yourself!”
Angrily I threw the coin into the center of the fountain.

The mood was spoiled. Why couldn’t a simple, such a good dream come true?

On returning home I was spinned by a whirlpool every day routine, which was sometimes brightened up with unexpected surprises. So, for tomorrow (finally, a day off) I would have planned a lot of pleasant business. I need to start decorating my recently purchased apartment... Or maybe should I start renovating the bathroom? I have to turn to professionals:
- Hello, Uncle Kolya? This is Lida. Hello! I’d like to consult with you...

My mother’s brother, the owner of a reputable construction company, knows everything about facing tiles and the repair.
- Have you got it? I’ll send my employee in the morning. The guy is smart. He will help.
We came to an agreement.

Imagine my surprise when at nine in the morning a guy, who had disrupted the flight of my coin, appeared at the fixed place! Not paying attention to my surprise, he began cheerfully:
- Good afternoon! Nikolai Ivanovich sends greetings to you!

When he looked closer at me, he was also amazed:
- You!?

My eyebrows, arched with indignation, trembled, and I, realizing the humor of the situation, laughed cheerfully. Well, you had to travel 350 km to come across! We live side by side.

“My name is Sasha,” the old acquaintance of mine introduced himself. - I will be helping you!
- Like that time?
- Oh, no, – he smiled. “It just happened so: I came to visit a friend for the weekend - we served together, he had been inviting me to visit for a long time. While he was at work settling his affairs, I decided to take a walk around the city - what a beauty! I saw you: a beautiful girl stood daydreaming. To attract your attention, I decided to toss a coin. And at that very moment you ...
- Oh, so you did this on purpose? – shouted I jokingly.
- Okay, let's treat each other familiarly.

A month later, my apartment was put in order, and I could safely invite friends. Sasha became the most frequent and most welcomed guest.

“I’m curious why didn’t the coin immediately fall into the water?” I thought, settling into Sasha’s strong embrace, “but the dream still came true...
– Do you want me to guess your wish?
- For sure!
Sasha kissed me tenderly and said quietly:
– You made a wish about me.
– You’re right, all girls dream of love...
