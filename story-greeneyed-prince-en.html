"Green-eyed prince" 💞

Author: Olga Kobylska | Translator: Helen Kapshytar | Theme: Destiny, White Horse & Fairy Tale 💕

After gloomy rainy days, the sky finally decided to pet us with some sunshine. The rays timidly shone through the clouds and tenderly stroked the tops of the trees, making them golden.

After grey monotony and crazy work rush, my soul wanted something clean, fresh and... extraordinary. And at the weekend I went to visit my mother in country.

- Nastenka! Such a surprise! – Mom was very happy to see me. - Oh, you look more beautiful than ever! Come on, let's go inside!

Having a delicious lunch, we were talking for a long time: mom about her news, I about mine. After having a rest, I decided to take a short walk.

- Why don’t you go into the garden and pick some apples? This year there are a lot of apples - the branches are breaking.

I took a small bag with me.

On the outskirts of the village there was a splendid, once being kolkhoz property, now ownerless orchard. Although no one had been working with trees for a long time, there was still enough fruit for the entire village. Look, what red-sided apples! One can take as much as he wants! Having picked half of the bag, I sat on a snag to admire the serenity of the old garden.

And suddenly a large red apple fell from a branch of a nearby tree - right at my feet. Then one more, another... What a strange hail of apples?

- Ku-ku! – came from above. A moment later, a young man landed in front of me. I gasped in surprise and stared at him.

- Hello! I am Ivan, Semenych’s grandson. Did I scare you a lot? - his face was cheerful, and his eyes were so extraordinary, green like young grass. Is he really the one I've been looking for all my life?

The thing is that back in school days (ten years ago), at Christmas time, the girls and I somehow got together to cast a tell fortunes. We were young and gay. But the blurred image of my betrothed, which I had seen in a dim mirror, stuck in my memory. It was a face with green eyes. My friends laughed, saying they had never seen such boys. And I made a wish - the groom will be green-eyed. Maybe that's why I’m still alone?

- Do you like apples too? – Ivan brought me out of my stupor, putting the plucked apples into my bag. “And I’ve already collected two bags.” Grandfather is making supplies for the winter.

We were talking and wandering around the autumn garden for a long time.

In the evening when I returned home with apples in my hands and a happy blush on my cheeks, my mother smiled:
“We’ll have to go to the garden tomorrow - we’ll make some jam.” –She nodded towards the window, where Ivan could be seen moving away.

The weekend flew by quickly, felt my life with apple aroma and happiness.

On Sunday evening, I sat in the car loaded with jars of sweet fruit. I cheerfully waved at my mother, promising that I would come back in five days.

Before reaching the clearing where the highway began, my car sneezed twice and stopped. For God’s sake! What should I do? There was nowhere to wait for help: at this time in the village, everyone was sitting at home. And there was about a kilometer and a half left to the highway. I'll probably go on foot. I slammed the door, zipped up my jacket, and put my bag over my shoulder. Let's go! And tomorrow I’ll come and pick up my “old lady”.

Having walked about a kilometer, I stopped to rest. It is getting dark so quickly - in an hour the sun will disappear. I have to hurry.

A barely audible crackling of branches made me turn around: wow! My green-eyed prince was racing along the path on a white horse. And they say that there are no fairy tales!

- Nastya! “Ivan jumped off his horse and embraced me.

It turned out that he was riding nearby and saw my car. He realized that I needed help - so he rushed to catch up with me.

- Get onto the Lord, we will get you to the city safe and sound.

On Wednesday, as I was getting ready to go home from the office, I noticed that my colleagues were discussing something animatedly, looking out the window over and over again. What was so interesting there?

Ivan stood on the curb near a brand-new silver Chevrolet with a huge bouquet of white roses.

– Who is this handsome guy waiting for? – whispered the girls.
- Hello!
- Hello, Nastya! Let’s go?
- How far are we going?
- With you - only in a fairy tale!
